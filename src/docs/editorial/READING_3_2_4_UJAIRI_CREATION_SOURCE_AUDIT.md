## Supporting recalls: discrepancies resolved against the original

- The three recalls blur **الحادث / الممكن**; the source's p. 95 footnote explicitly calls them independent proofs.
- All three adopt the prisoner interpretation as though it were Ibn Kathir's preferred reading. Recall 2 also reverses the first/second-stage relation. The original reports the former and then prefers another reading; the first stage prepares the second.
- Recall 3 constructs a four-way al-Tur scheme. The original rejects two origin alternatives, establishes the creator, then demonstrates incapacity after creation.
- Definitions of regress as an infinite temporal past conflict with the original's explicit discussion of a beginningless genus. The revised prose and aids preserve the difference between causal dependence and succession of created individuals.
- Recall 3 confuses Ibn Hazm's four subordinate self-creation cases with proofs of the world's beginning. The source begins that passage after establishing a beginning.
- The recalls contain inflated certainty, reconstructed quotations and added discovery details. These are not primary authority. Only verified short quotations remain quoted; other treatments are attributed paraphrases.
- Statements that thermal death is prevented or impossible change the source's argument from its not having occurred. The latter is retained.
