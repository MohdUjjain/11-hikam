## Resolved fidelity issues

1. **Vitz:** all three Recalls turn the Oedipal reply into an accepted or proven theory. P059/p. 27 expressly marks it as إلزام and rejects adopting it as his true explanation. The visible chapter, Companion and revision preserve that distinction. P057's psychological premise retains **بالأساس** under Vitz attribution.
2. **Invented father mapping:** Recall 3 assigns named people to three formal father categories and adds Nietzsche. The original lists Freud, Voltaire, Marx, Hitler and Feuerbach without that mapping. Only the supplied examples remain.
3. **Bouazza:** three causes are his; P090 explicitly introduces Azmi's own science argument. It is not a fourth Bouazza cause.
4. **Source certainty:** Shahri's susceptibility/crisis conditional and Azmi's clear cosmic evidence retain their stated confidence. The social argument preserves atheism together with unrestrained individual utility in its premise, and faith as the author's stated guarantee of cohesion.
5. **Eight study findings:** these remain Azmi's account of the 2004 article. No independent paper review, study design, sample, author names or new certainty labels are claimed. Preserve **more children**, not a childless/parent substitution; preserve **علاج مناسب**, not **العلاج المناسب**. The scan resolves native **أغلى** to **أعلى**.
6. **Two closing voices:** the forum letter and the inserted social-media screenshot are distinct. Recall 1 and Recall 3 blend them into a false quotation. No combined quotation remains; the letter's argumentative role is conveyed in academic paraphrase.
7. **No invented psychological concession:** neither the closing body nor notes 78–83 supplies the concession claimed by the earlier prepared-source ledger. The actual express boundaries are P109's external-considerations/proof distinction and P059's conditional reply to Freud. The former is taught without displacing the positive arguments through repeated cautionary framing.
8. **Other recall additions:** no «إمام الحرمين الغزالي», invented counterpart epistemology table, “absolute” clinical certainty or characterization of انفكاك الجهة itself as the fallacy has been imported.
9. **Transcription versus outside correction:** the scan confirms «العقلية», «للمعسكر الآخر» and the numbered seventh advocacy trait. The source really prints «حزب الأفكار» and calls Pascal «اللاأدري»; these are not silently rewritten into different quoted wording. The nonessential labels are not used as independent biographical assertions. The diagnostic maxim is presented as a rule Azmi reports, without claiming independent verification of its named originator's credentials.
